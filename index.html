DangerHarvest Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Rayven Studio operates the DangerHarvest Android app.

Information we collect and use

We collect player and device identifiers, technical information, and gameplay activity to operate the game, understand its use and improve the experience. Game progress and preferences are stored on your device. If you contact us, we also receive the information you provide.

Service providers

We use Amplitude Session Replay to understand the early gameplay experience. For eligible sessions, it reconstructs on-screen game content and interactions during onboarding and the first four levels. Recording stops after level 4 completion or later mobile-level progress and remains off for that installation. Player ID text is masked in replays, and input masking is enabled. Replays are associated with analytics session and installation identifiers.

We use Cloudflare for backend services and Google Analytics for Firebase and Amplitude for analytics. These providers process information in connection with those services, which may involve processing outside your country. We may also disclose information when required by law.

Data storage and protection

We use encrypted connections to protect information during transmission. Player records are retained until manually deleted, and analytics data follows our service providers’ retention settings. To request deletion, contact ghanasyamkr@gmail.com.

Your choices and requests

The app currently collects analytics automatically without an in-app opt-out.

You can request access to or deletion of your information by emailing ghanasyamkr@gmail.com. Include your Player ID, if available. We may need to verify your request.

We handle deletion requests manually, including requests involving our service providers, subject to applicable legal requirements. Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete previously collected server or analytics data.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy and revise the date above. We will provide additional notice where required.

For privacy questions or requests, contact Rayven Studio at ghanasyamkr@gmail.com.
